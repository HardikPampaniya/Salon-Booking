# Meta creative brief

## Brand and audience

Create clear, warm, local ads for The Dream Beauty Unisex Studio in Karanjade, Navi Mumbai. The salon is unisex, so start with all genders. Use an adult audience; proposed test age is 18–45, subject to Meta's controls and the owner's audience approval.

## Candidate local assets

The project contains these salon-provided local assets. They are candidates only until the owner confirms paid-ad usage rights and exact intended category:

- Interior: `client/public/images/gallery/salon-interior.png`, `mirror-stations.png`, `workstations.png`.
- Women/hair: `client/public/images/services/womens-hair-services.jpg`, `womens-hair-finish.png`.
- Men: `client/public/images/services/mens-grooming.jpg`, `mens-styling-station.png`.
- Beauty: `client/public/images/services/beauty-facial.jpg`, `beauty-studio-mirrors.png`.

Do not use CSS placeholders, scraped images, or customer review text as paid creative. Ask the owner to approve the actual image files and any staff/customer appearing in them.

## Layouts to prepare

- **Feed:** create a 4:5 vertical master (1080 × 1350 px is a practical working canvas) with the subject centered, short copy, and generous safe margins. Make a square crop only if Ads Manager placement preview needs it.
- **Stories/Reels:** create a separate 9:16 master (1080 × 1920 px working canvas). Keep text and logos away from top and bottom interface controls. Use a 10–15 second vertical walk-through or 3–4 simple cuts if the owner provides video; use captions because many viewers watch muted.
- **Facebook Feed:** use the same clean 4:5 version as a starting point, then preview the actual placement crop. Meta can crop image assets by placement; do not assume one crop will fit every surface.

These are production canvas recommendations, not a promise of Meta's current upload requirements. Check Ads Manager's placement preview and upload validation before publishing.

## Concept visuals

1. **Meet the studio:** actual interior photo, salon name, “Karanjade · Navi Mumbai,” and one short service line.
2. **Category menu:** one approved women/hair, men/grooming, or beauty photo; category heading; “Explore services.” Do not combine unrelated photos into one mismatched collage.
3. **Book a visit (conditional):** actual service photo, date/time cue, and “₹100 advance credited toward your service bill.” Use only after payment and appointment confirmation work on the live domain.

## Production checks

- Owner approves the image, crop, overlay, caption, price statements, offer dates, and final destination URL.
- Keep text contrast high and the main subject visible in both feed and vertical crops.
- Avoid altered before/after comparisons, skin-tone retouching claims, guaranteed outcomes, body-shaming language, and any unverified rating or testimonial.
- Preview feed, Stories, and Reels separately inside Ads Manager before submitting.
