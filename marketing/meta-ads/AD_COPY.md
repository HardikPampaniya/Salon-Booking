# Draft Meta ad copy

These drafts use only the studio name, Karanjade location, and broad service categories already in the project. They contain no unapproved discount, result guarantee, rating, or customer testimonial. The owner must approve each final ad and photo before submission.

## Concept 1 — Meet the studio

**Primary text (English)**  
Looking for a salon in Karanjade? The Dream Beauty Unisex Studio offers hair, beauty, and grooming services. Browse the menu and contact the studio to plan your visit.

**Primary text (Hinglish)**  
Karanjade mein salon dhoondh rahe hain? The Dream Beauty Unisex Studio mein hair, beauty aur grooming services dekhiye. Menu explore karein aur apni visit plan karne ke liye studio se baat karein.

**Headline:** Hair, beauty & grooming in Karanjade  
**Description:** Explore the studio menu and plan a visit.  
**CTA:** Learn More. If appointment bookings are confirmed available, use Book Now.  
**Destination:** `https://dreambeautystudio.in/services` while booking is off; `/book` only after the launch gate passes.  
**Visual:** Owner-approved wide or vertical photo of the actual salon interior, with small studio name and “Karanjade” label.

From hair care to beauty and grooming, explore the services listed at The Dream Beauty Unisex Studio in Karanjade. Check the current menu and contact the studio with questions about a service.

**Primary text (Hinglish)**  
Hair care se beauty aur grooming tak, The Dream Beauty Unisex Studio, Karanjade ka service menu dekhiye. Kisi service ke baare mein sawaal ho toh studio se contact karein.

**Headline:** Explore the salon service menu  
**Description:** Hair, beauty, and grooming in Karanjade.  
**CTA:** Learn More.  
**Destination:** Match the ad to `https://dreambeautystudio.in/services/women`, `/services/beauty`, or `/services/men`.

## Concept 3 — Appointment-focused

Use this only after online booking and live payment have been tested successfully.

**Primary text (English)**  
Plan your visit to The Dream Beauty Unisex Studio in Karanjade. Choose an available date and one-hour arrival window during salon hours. A ₹100 advance confirms the booking and is credited toward your service bill.

**Primary text (Hinglish)**  
Karanjade ke The Dream Beauty Unisex Studio mein apni visit plan karein. Salon hours ke andar available date aur one-hour arrival window chunein. ₹100 advance se booking confirm hoti hai aur yeh service bill mein adjust hota hai.

**Headline:** Choose a date and arrival time  
**Description:** ₹100 advance is credited toward the service bill.  
**CTA:** Book Now.  
**Destination:** `https://dreambeautystudio.in/book`.  
**Visual:** An owner-approved salon/service photograph plus a simple “Choose your visit time” overlay. Show the ₹100 advance and credit wording legibly; do not imply that ₹100 is the full service price.
